Recording rules the screens follow

Tasks 1 to 5 are sustained vowels (ah, eh, ee, oh, oo). Tasks 6 to 8 are syllable repeats (pa, ta, ka). Every recording uses a count-up timer. It stops by itself after 1.5 s of silence, or at a hard cap of 30 s, with no countdown or warning shown. Recording begins about 0.5 s before "Go" so the room noise can be measured; nothing visual changes at that point.

Coach checks (placeholders to calibrate)

Too quiet: loudest point below about -34 dBFS. Too loud: more than 0.1% of samples at maximum. Too noisy: speech less than 15 dB above room noise. Too short: less than 3 s of sound, which is a soft prompt only. After two short tries the screen leads with "Use this recording". The coach never comments on health, and every state offers a way to continue.

Build notes

Layout uses View, Text and Pressable only. The countdown ring is 60 small absolutely positioned Views rotated around the ring center (no SVG library). Waveform bars are 27 Views driven by one shared loudness value. Use tabular numerals (fontVariant: tabular-nums) on the timer so digits do not jitter. Read Reduce Motion with Reanimated's useReducedMotion and re-check it when the setting changes.
